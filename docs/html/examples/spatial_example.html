Spatial Transcriptomics Analysis

This example runs Ageas on spatial transcriptomics (ST) data from a regenerating tissue section. It assembles an AnnData object with spatial coordinates from the raw count and annotation tables, applies quality-control filtering and clustering, and selects the cell clusters that make up a candidate lineage. n_kfold_selection over a Hangar panel is then used to infer the fate bias separating the lineage, and the predicted probabilities are visualized in both spatial and UMAP embeddings.
