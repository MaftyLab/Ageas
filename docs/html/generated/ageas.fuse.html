ageas.fuse

ageas.fuse(vote: numpy.ndarray, walk: numpy.ndarray, weight: float = 0.6) → numpy.ndarray

Two-class probabilities from the vote and the walk.

Cells are ranked by (1 - weight) * rank(vote) + weight * rank(walk) (ranks of the second class’s probability, scaled to (0, 1]), and the walk’s mean probability of the second class sets how many are called it. The returned probability is a cell’s place in that ranking, rescaled so that exactly those cells lie above 0.5 (0.01-0.99): a ranking with an estimated share, not a per-cell calibration.
