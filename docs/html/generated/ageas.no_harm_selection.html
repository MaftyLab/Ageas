ageas.no_harm_selection

ageas.no_harm_selection(oof_probs: dict, labels, min_relative_gain: float = 0.01, max_steps: int = 50, weights: numpy.ndarray = None) → dict

Weights of the units that improve the ensemble’s OOF log-loss.

Starts from the best single unit and repeatedly adds whichever unit (repeats allowed) lowers the OOF log-loss of the count-weighted mean the most, until the best addition gains less than min_relative_gain of the current loss. Then, while some unit’s removal (all of its copies) would not raise the log-loss by at least that fraction, the unit whose removal hurts least is dropped. Every unit that stays therefore improves the ensemble by at least min_relative_gain of its loss. Gains are relative because terminal-cell losses can be tiny, which would make any fixed threshold either meaningless or impossible to clear.

Parameters:

  • oof_probs – Mapping unit_id -> (n_cells, n_classes) OOF probabilities, rows aligned with labels.

  • labels – Integer class label of every cell.

  • min_relative_gain – Smallest log-loss improvement a unit must bring, both to join and to stay, as a fraction of the ensemble’s current loss.

  • max_steps – Largest number of additions, repeats included.

  • weights – Optional weight of each cell in the log-loss, e.g. the shift-aware validation weights; None counts every cell alike.

Returns:

Mapping unit_id -> weight over the selected units; the weights sum to one.
