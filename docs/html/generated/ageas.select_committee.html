ageas.select_committee

ageas.select_committee(query_probs: dict, ranked: list, max_jsd: float = 0.01, min_majority: float = 0.5) → list

The committee drawn from ranked units, judged on their query probabilities.

  1. Walking ranked best first, a unit joins unless its class probabilities differ from an existing member’s by less than max_jsd bits on average over the query cells: such a pair is one view counted twice.

  2. With three or more members, a member is dropped when its call matches the call of the other members’ average on fewer than min_majority of the query cells: it has collapsed or flipped where the rest have not. Every member is judged against the same committee, in one pass.

Parameters:

  • query_probs – unit_id -> (n_cells, n_classes) probabilities on the query cells.

  • ranked – Candidate units, best first.

  • max_jsd – Mean Jensen-Shannon divergence (bits) below which two units count as one.

  • min_majority – Smallest share of query cells on which a member must side with the rest of the committee.

Returns:

Member unit IDs, best first.
